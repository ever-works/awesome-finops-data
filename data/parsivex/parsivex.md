## Overview

Parsivex is an AWS cost waste scanning service that identifies idle and over-provisioned resources.

## Features

- Scans for idle EC2 instances
- Detects unattached EBS volumes
- Identifies oversized RDS databases
- Finds stale snapshots
- Detects NAT gateway overuse
- Monthly waste totals with category breakdown

## Pricing

- Free tier: monthly scan for one AWS account with total monthly waste and category breakdown
- Paid plans: itemized findings and scheduled re-scans

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS.
