## Overview

CloudHealth is an enterprise-grade, policy-driven cloud cost governance platform. Following Broadcom's acquisition of VMware's CloudHealth division, the platform offers enterprise features but introduced some uncertainty around the roadmap and pricing. It provides solid multi-cloud visibility and customizable governance policies.

## Key Features

- Enterprise-grade policy-driven cost governance
- Solid multi-cloud visibility across AWS, Azure, and GCP
- Customizable governance policies
- Budget tracking and forecasting
- Cost allocation for finance teams
- Executive reporting and dashboards
- Integration with ITSM tools and financial systems

## Platform Engineering Fit

CloudHealth is best suited for organizations that prioritize financial governance and executive visibility over developer self-service. It integrates better with ITSM tools and financial systems than developer portals and GitOps workflows. Platform engineers may find fewer developer-facing features compared to cloud-native and developer-focused alternatives.

## Pricing Model

Paid enterprise platform. Pricing varies based on cloud spend and features. Contact Broadcom/VMware for specific pricing.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform with multi-cloud support.
