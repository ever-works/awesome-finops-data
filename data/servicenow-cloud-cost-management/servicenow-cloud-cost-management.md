## Overview

ServiceNow Cloud Cost Management integrates FinOps capabilities into the ServiceNow IT service management (ITSM) platform. It connects cost optimization workflows with existing incident management, change requests, and service catalogs, creating workflow continuity for organizations already using ServiceNow.

## Key Features

- Cost optimization requests flow through existing ServiceNow approval workflows
- Change management connects infrastructure changes to cost impacts
- Service catalog displays cost estimates for resource requests
- Integration with ITSM processes and enterprise governance
- Audit trails and compliance reporting
- Cost allocation and chargeback capabilities

## Platform Engineering Fit

ServiceNow excels at enterprise process management rather than developer experience. Approval-heavy workflows may introduce friction for self-service platforms. It is best suited for regulated industries that prioritize governance and audit trails over development velocity. The platform represents a workflow-based approach to remediation rather than autonomous optimization.

Cloud-based SaaS platform, deployed as part of the ServiceNow ITSM platform.
